# When to post YouTube Shorts and TikTok for a Vietnamese audience (GMT+7), 2025–2026

Research date: 2026-11-10. About 20 tool calls were used. Several primary pages could not be fetched: MediaPost returned 403, one Buffer page returned 500, the TikTok Support analytics page loaded only navigation, and DataReportal's Vietnam 2026 tables did not load. Those gaps are marked below. Labels used throughout: **[Platform]** means the platform confirmed it, **[3rd-party]** means a third-party dataset or estimate, and **[Inference]** is my own reasoning.

## 1. What large 2025–2026 datasets say about best TikTok and YouTube Shorts times

### Takeaway
The big studies do not agree. Buffer 2026 favours weekend mornings and evenings for TikTok (Sunday 9:00, Saturday best overall day). Sprout Social 2026 says weekdays 13:00–18:00 and avoid weekends. For Shorts, Metricool 2026 says 14:00–18:00 and SocialPilot 2026 says 12:00–14:00 / 18:00–19:00, with Thursday to Saturday best. Sprout and SocialPilot state their times in the audience's local time, so they apply directly in GMT+7. Buffer's time-zone handling is not explained. None of these datasets is Vietnam-specific.

### Cited Findings
**TikTok**
- [3rd-party] **Buffer 2026:** 7.1M TikTok posts published through Buffer, measured by median engagement rate. Article dated 14 Sep 2026; the date range of the posts is not given. Top slots are **Sunday 9 a.m., Monday 1 p.m., Sunday 1 p.m.** The best day is **Saturday**, then Monday, then Sunday. Best hour by day: Mon 13:00, Tue 06:00, Wed 22:00, Thu 13:00, Fri 18:00, Sat 17:00, Sun 09:00. "Engagement generally peaks between 6 p.m. and 11 p.m."; afternoons (12–5 p.m.) are generally lowest, with some activity at lunchtime. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok)
- [3rd-party] **Buffer time zone:** Buffer says its data team made "the recommended timezones universally applicable" but does not explain how. Buffer also recommends checking TikTok Studio > Followers > "Most active times" first. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok)
- [3rd-party] **Earlier Buffer study:** a 1M-post Buffer analysis (Oct 2025) gave different top slots (Sunday 20:00, 17:00, 16:00). This shows how much the "best times" shift between datasets. — [iqfluence summary](https://iqfluence.io/public/blog/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok) (secondary, not verified on Buffer)
- [3rd-party] **Sprout Social 2026:** nearly 2B engagements across about 307K profiles, all networks combined (no TikTok-only sample size). Data period 27 Nov 2025 – 27 Feb 2026, published 31 Mar 2026. Times are in **local time**. TikTok best times: Mon 15–17h, Tue 14–18h, Wed 13–20h, Thu 13–17h, Fri 15–17h. Worst: **weekends** and early mornings. — [Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-social-media/)
- Buffer and Sprout directly conflict on weekends (Buffer: Saturday and Sunday strong; Sprout: avoid weekends). Buffer measures engagement rate on posts scheduled mostly by SMB/creator accounts. Sprout measures engagement on business profiles. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok); [Sprout](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-social-media/)

**YouTube / Shorts**
- [3rd-party] **Metricool 2026 YouTube study:** 799,718 videos from 71,177 channels. Shorts perform best **14:00–18:00**; long-form does best 10:00–16:00. The page does not state a time zone. — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/best-time-to-post-on-youtube/)
- [3rd-party] **SocialPilot 2026:** 301K+ videos from 27K+ channels, all formats (no Shorts-only sample given). Times are in the **viewer's local time**. Best Shorts days are **Thursday, Friday, Saturday**. Upload windows are **12–14h or 18–19h**, with viewer peaks at 12–15h and 19–21h. The strongest single window is Friday 16–19h. Avoid 3–7h and Sunday after 20:00. Day-by-day windows: Mon 7h/12h; Tue 7–11h/17–19h; Wed 12h/15–18h; Thu 19–23h; Fri 7–9h/11–13h; Sat 9–10h/15–18h; Sun 10–11h/15–17h. The article contradicts itself in places (Friday peak given as 4–6 PM + 9–11 PM in a table, 4–7 PM in the text). — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/insights/best-time-to-post-on-youtube)
- [3rd-party] **Other Shorts claims (secondary, unverified):**
  - A blog reports that a 2025 Metricool study found Shorts posted at **01:00** got the highest average views (3,184). — [FlowShorts](https://flowshorts.app/blog/best-time-to-post-youtube-shorts)
  - Hopper HQ: Tuesday 11:00–12:00 local. — [Hopper HQ](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/best-time-to-post-youtube-shorts/)
  - Adobe: analysed 24,274 Shorts from 280 creators; findings not retrieved. — [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/best-times-to-post-youtube-shorts)
  - Blogs attribute Buffer "4 PM" YouTube slots (Mon, Wed or Fri depending on the blog). These seem to come from general YouTube data, not Shorts-specific data. — [The Leap](https://www.theleap.co/blog/best-time-to-post-on-youtube-shorts/)
- Hootsuite and Later 2025–2026 editions were not retrieved. See Gaps.

### Inferences
- [Inference] Sprout and SocialPilot use local time, so their windows read directly as GMT+7 times for a Vietnamese audience. That holds only if Vietnamese daily routines resemble those of the (mostly Western) accounts in the samples, which is unproven.
- [Inference] Across the studies there is weak agreement on two windows: **around 12–14h** (lunch) and **18–22h** (evening). Late morning (11:30/11:45) is not singled out by any study as a peak for Shorts or TikTok. The one exception is Hopper's Tuesday 11–12h, and SocialPilot lists Friday 11–13h.

### Gaps
- Hootsuite 2025/2026 and Later 2025/2026 TikTok/YouTube numbers were not retrieved.
- No Shorts-only sample size was confirmed for any study, and no study has a Vietnam or Asia breakdown.

## 2. What YouTube and TikTok officially say about posting time

### Takeaway
YouTube's Shorts product lead has said Shorts does not prioritize any time of day; timing matters mainly for news or topical content. YouTube's own "When your viewers are on YouTube" report is officially framed for Premieres and live streams, not for Shorts uploads. I could not retrieve an official TikTok text on Follower activity. Third-party guides describe a TikTok Studio Followers tab with active hours, but disagree on its time zone (UTC or device time).

### Cited Findings
- [Platform, via press] **Todd Sherman, YouTube Shorts product lead, on Creator Insider (Aug 2023, older than the 2025 cut-off):**
  - Shorts does not prioritize specific times of day. Timeliness matters for news, which is "less about time of day and more about quickly responding."
  - Posting frequency, specific timing and reposting don't necessarily boost performance.
  - Nothing in the algorithm boosts an account for uploading 10–20 Shorts a day, and doing so could look like spam.
  - — [Benzinga](https://benzinga.com/z/34020532); [Metricool ES summary](https://metricool.com/es/algoritmo-youtube-shorts); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/388588/) (could not be opened, 403)
- [Platform] **YouTube Help, "Understand your YouTube audience":** the "When your viewers are on YouTube" report shows "when your viewers are online across YouTube in the last 28 days." It can help "understand when to schedule a Premiere, or to plan your next live stream." The page says nothing about time zone and gives no posting-time guidance for Shorts. Viewer metrics include Shorts. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314416)
- [3rd-party] **TikTok Studio > Analytics > Followers > "Follower activity":** shows the hours and days followers are most active. It reportedly needs a Creator or Business account. Display time zone is reported as UTC, or as device local time depending on app version, so check it before scheduling. — [MeetEdgar](https://meetedgar.com/blog/the-best-time-to-post-on-tiktok); [Printify](https://printify.com/blog/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok/)
- [3rd-party] Buffer also points to TikTok Studio "Most active times" and says content quality and the hook matter more than timing. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok)
- [3rd-party] SocialPilot notes that Shorts can resurface days or weeks after posting, so consistency matters more than exact timing. — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/insights/best-time-to-post-on-youtube)

### Inferences
- [Inference] On both platforms, distribution comes mostly from the recommendation feed (Shorts feed, For You), not from subscribers. Upload time mainly affects the first few hours: subscriber/follower notifications and early signals. That is consistent with Sherman's statement. Expect gains of a few percent from timing, not multiples.
- [Inference] The YouTube viewers-online heatmap is still the best channel-specific proxy, even though Google frames it for Premieres and lives.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve TikTok's own Help Center or Creator Academy wording on Follower activity or its time zone; support.tiktok.com loaded only navigation.
- I found no official 2025–2026 YouTube statement updating Sherman's 2023 remarks.

## 3. Vietnam-specific usage peaks by hour

### Takeaway
I found no 2025–2026 survey that measures Vietnamese hourly peaks per platform. The best available data is older: 2023 RMIT/Adtima, with 20–22h as the best ad window and 18–20h second, and with lunch, afternoon and evening named as social/chat peaks. 2025 data covers volume only: about 7.3 h/day on smartphones, more than 41 h/month on TikTok. Vietnamese work culture has a long midday break (around 12:00–13:30).

### Cited Findings
- [3rd-party, 2023] **RMIT x Adtima whitepaper:** social/chat platforms are used more in the evening, at noon and in the afternoon. **20:00–22:00** is the best time of day to advertise to Vietnamese users, followed by **18:00–20:00**. — [RMIT](https://www.rmit.edu.vn/vi/tin-tuc/tat-ca-tin-tuc/2023/sep/ven-man-do-hot-cua-chat-va-mang-xa-hoi-o-viet-nam); [Advertising Vietnam](https://advertisingvietnam.com/article/khung-gio-vang-dang-bai-tren-5-nen-tang-mang-xa-hoi-pho-bien-nhat-2023-giup-thuong-hieu-gia-tang-co-hoi-tiep-can-nguoi-dung-p22627)
- [3rd-party, 2025] **Q&Me (iPhone screen-time data, HCMC and Hanoi, March 2025, not nationally representative):** average smartphone use is 7.3 h/day, of which 43% is social media (down from 47%). Zalo rose to #2 in time spent. Facebook, Zalo, TikTok, YouTube and Messenger take about 80% of time. — [Advertising Vietnam](https://advertisingvietnam.com/article/muc-do-pho-bien-cua-ung-dung-di-dong-viet-nam-nam-2025-nguoi-viet-danh-trung-binh-7-3-gio-ngay-de-dung-smartphone-zalo-la-ung-dung-tin-nhan-duoc-su-dung-nhieu-nhat-p26311); [Brands Vietnam](https://www.brandsvietnam.com/congdong/topic/q-me-ung-dung-di-dong-pho-bien-nhat-tai-viet-nam-nam-2025)
- [3rd-party, 2025] **We Are Social / Meltwater, Digital 2025 Vietnam:** Vietnamese users spend more than 41 h/month on TikTok and more than 20 h/month on Facebook. — [Brands Vietnam](https://www.brandsvietnam.com/library/doc/68255b3eb7b90-we-are-social-vietnam-digital-2025)
- [3rd-party, 2025] YouTube's ad reach in Vietnam fell by about 0.7M (-1.1%) from Jan 2024 to Jan 2025. The source warns that ad reach is not MAU and the drop may reflect data adjustments. — [SmartAds, Digital Insight 2025](https://smartads.tech/blog/detail/digital-insight-2025-vietnam-report-what-do-marketers-need-to-do-to-break-through--Fi4tFVBYd1NcWQ==.html)
- [3rd-party] DataReportal's Digital 2026 Vietnam report exists, but its tables did not load. A secondary summary gives about 85.6M internet users and 79M social media users. — [DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-vietnam); [Vietstock](https://en.vietstock.vn/2026/02/digital-landscape-shows-shift-toward-experience-led-competition-report-says-38-629443.htm)
- [3rd-party, date unclear] **Q&Me "Digital life" (via Tiền Phong):** 2h52 a day on social media. About 45% of 18–34-year-olds check social media right after waking up and right before sleeping. — [Tiền Phong](https://tienphong.vn/post-1729859.html)
- [3rd-party, 2019] An Apota survey found 75% of Vietnamese pick up their phone within 15 minutes of waking. — [Tuổi Trẻ](https://tuoitre.vn/75-nguoi-viet-vua-thuc-day-la-vo-lay-dien-thoai-20190915083938502.htm)
- [3rd-party, low quality, no data cited] **Scheduling-tool blogs:**
  - Vietnamese offices often pause around 12:00–13:30 for lunch and rest. — [timetopost.co](https://timetopost.co/best-time-to-post/facebook/vietnam-small-business/)
  - Best window for the Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh time zone is 19:00–21:00. — [Radaar](https://www.radaar.io/free-tools/best-times-to-post/asia-ho-chi-minh/)

### Inferences
- [Inference] Vietnam likely has a bimodal pattern: a lunch/nap peak (about 11:30–13:30, when office workers and students are on their break) and a larger evening peak (about 19:00–22:30). Morning wake-up checking (06:00–07:30) is a smaller third window. The 11:30–11:45 slots currently in use sit at the start of the lunch peak, which is defensible for adults.

## 4. When Vietnamese children aged 5–9 and their parents watch online video

### Takeaway
I found no survey that measures the hours at which Vietnamese children watch YouTube or TikTok. The school schedule points to after school (around 16:30–18:00) and the evening (around 19:00–21:00) on weekdays, plus weekend mornings. Most primary pupils are at school over lunch, because 2-session days with semi-boarding lunch and nap are standard, and free 2-session teaching was rolled out from 2025–2026. An 11:30 weekday slot therefore misses children; it reaches parents on their lunch break.

### Cited Findings
- [Official/press] **Primary school day:** the Ministry of Education (MOET) guidance allows at most 7 periods a day at primary and lower-secondary level, and primary periods are 35 minutes long. A typical school has 4 periods in the morning, lunch and rest at midday, and 2–3 periods in the afternoon, so many days pupils leave school **after 15:00**. — [Tiền Phong](https://tienphong.vn/day-hoc-2-buoi-ngay-phu-huynh-kho-vi-don-con-truong-loay-hoay-tim-giai-phap-post1778389.html); [VOH](https://voh.com.vn/giao-duc/truong-tieu-hoc-day-khong-qua-7-tietngay-moi-tiet-35-phut-545376.html)
- [Press] **Lunch break:** in Hải Phòng, semi-boarding primary schools close their gates **11:30–13:30** for the midday break. — [PLO](https://plo.vn/yeu-cau-bo-tri-khu-vuc-cho-cho-hoc-sinh-khong-ban-tru-post576686.html)
- [Press, 2025] Free 2-session-a-day teaching for primary and lower-secondary is being rolled out from school year 2025–2026. — [Tuổi Trẻ](https://tuoitre.vn/day-2-buoi-ngay-khong-thu-phi-cho-som-co-huong-dan-20250507230007763.htm); [Viettimes](https://viettimes.vn/thong-nhat-day-2-buoingay-mien-phi-tu-nam-hoc-2025-2026-post185298.html)
- [Press, anecdote, 2021] One HCMC family lets their child watch YouTube for 30 minutes after coming home from school, plus 30 minutes in the morning during holidays. — [Tuổi Trẻ](https://tuoitre.vn/giai-tri-o-nha-co-nao-cung-dung-youtube-20210525093302095.htm)
- [Press] Parents struggle to supervise children's internet use when both work all day. — [VietnamNet](https://vietnamnet.vn/en/parental-concerns-grow-over-children-s-internet-use-2372118.html)
- [Google, older] The top 40 kids' channels in Vietnam draw more than 550M views a month. — [Google VN blog 2017](https://vietnamese.googleblog.com/2017/05/xu-huong-tim-kiem-tai-viet-nam-cai-gi.html)

### Inferences
- [Inference] For 5–9-year-olds watching with a parent, the realistic weekday windows are **17:00–18:30** (after pickup, before dinner) and **19:00–20:30** (after dinner, before bed around 21:00). On weekends, **08:00–10:30** and late afternoon. Summer holidays (June–August) and Tết shift viewing into the daytime.
- [Inference] If the channel is set to "Made for Kids", personalisation and some features such as notifications are reduced (this is from memory and was not verified this session). That makes posting time matter even less for YouTube. Check YouTube Help before relying on it.

### Gaps
- I found no measured data on the hours at which Vietnamese children watch video, from UNICEF, MIC/MoIC, Kantar or Google.
- I found no data on YouTube Kids vs main-app usage in Vietnam.

## 5. Posting frequency and consistency

### Takeaway
On TikTok, Buffer's 11.4M-post study shows views per post rise with frequency, with the biggest jump going from 1 post a week to 2–5. The median post stays around 500 views at every frequency, so the gain shows up in the top-performing posts. On YouTube, Shorts frequency itself gives no algorithmic boost (Sherman, 2023). Third-party guidance suggests 3–7 Shorts a week. Daily posting is fine if quality holds.

### Cited Findings
- [3rd-party] **Buffer frequency study (Oct 2025):** 11.4M TikTok posts from 150K+ accounts. Compared with posting once a week:
  - 2–5 posts/week: **+17%** views per post
  - 6–10 posts/week: **+29%**
  - 11+ posts/week: **+34%**

  Median views stay around 500 at every frequency. The 90th percentile rises from 3,722 views (1/week) to 6,983 (2–5/week) to 14,401 (11+/week). Buffer's data scientist recommends starting with 2–5 posts a week. The data is observational and skews toward SMB accounts. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/study-shows-2-5-weekly-tiktoks-deliver-biggest-view-increase/558641/)
- [Platform, 2023] YouTube: no algorithmic boost for uploading many Shorts a day; mass uploading may look like spam. — [Benzinga](https://benzinga.com/z/34020532)
- [3rd-party] **YouTube upload frequency:** Metricool 2026 says 2–4 long-form uploads a week is the "sweet spot." For Shorts, a higher frequency can work because Shorts rely on discovery, but quality comes first. — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/best-time-to-post-on-youtube/)
- [3rd-party] SocialPilot recommends 3–5 Shorts a week in one section and 5–7 a week in another. These are recommendations, not findings from its data. — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/insights/best-time-to-post-on-youtube)

### Inferences
- [Inference] A daily post (7 a week) sits in Buffer's "6–10" band, which is consistent with continued per-post gains. Daily posting is justified as long as production quality and retention don't drop. Consistency of time mainly helps returning viewers build a habit, and it makes A/B testing interpretable.

### Gaps
- I found no official TikTok statement on optimal frequency for 2025–2026.

## 6. Two series posting 15 minutes apart (11:30 and 11:45): same or separate channels, and spacing

### Takeaway
I found no official or large-dataset guidance on how far apart uploads on one channel should be. The two series serve audiences with different viewing hours. Kids watching with parents (evening, weekends) and 16–35-year-olds (lunch and late evening) argue for separate time slots, and arguably separate channels, rather than back-to-back noon posts.

### Cited Findings
- No source found on minimum spacing between uploads. The only related official remark is that frequency and timing don't necessarily boost Shorts performance (Sherman, 2023). — [Benzinga](https://benzinga.com/z/34020532)

### Inferences
- [Inference] **Same channel:** the two posts compete for the same subscriber notifications and the same early-feed test pool. Mixing a kids' science audience with a young-adult idiom audience also dilutes the channel's audience signal for recommendations. If they stay on one channel, separate them by several hours and put each in its own audience window.
- [Inference] **Kids' content and "Made for Kids":** the kids' series will likely have to be declared Made for Kids under COPPA and YouTube policy, which limits comments and personalised features. That is a strong reason to keep it on a separate channel or account from the 16–35 series. Verify on YouTube Help.

### Gaps
- I found no data on whether same-channel uploads within 15 minutes cannibalise each other.

## Recommended schedules and testing method (all [Inference], built from the findings above)

**"Ông Ơi, Vì Sao?" (kids 5–9 with parents)**
- Main slot: **weekdays 18:30**, so the video is indexed before the 19:00–20:30 family window. Alternative: 17:00.
- **Weekends 08:30.**
- Frequency: daily or 5 a week. Keep the same slot on YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Note that TikTok requires users to be 13+, so the TikTok audience is the parent; test 20:00 there.

**"Nói Vậy Mà Không Phải Vậy" (16–35)**
- Option A: **12:00–12:15**, at the core of the lunch break rather than its edge. 11:45 is acceptable.
- Option B: **20:30–21:30**, the evening peak per RMIT 2023 and the 6–11 p.m. pattern in Buffer 2026.
- Test A against B. On TikTok, also test a weekend slot (Buffer favours weekends, Sprout does not), for example Saturday 17:00 or Sunday 09:00.
- Frequency: daily is fine (Buffer's 6–10/week band).

**Testing protocol**
1. Check analytics first:
   - YouTube Studio > Analytics > Audience > "When your viewers are on YouTube" (last 28 days).
   - TikTok Studio > Analytics > Followers > Follower activity. Confirm whether it shows UTC (then add 7 hours) or device time.
   - Post about 30–60 minutes before the darkest block.
2. Run each slot for **2–3 weeks**, at least 10–14 posts per slot, changing only the time and keeping content types rotating evenly.
3. Compare **medians, not means**: views at 24 hours and 7 days, % "viewed vs swiped away" (Shorts), average view duration / % watched, and new followers per post. Viral outliers distort averages, and Buffer shows medians barely move.
4. Expect small differences from timing. Keep the slot that wins on median 24-hour views and retention. Re-check every quarter and around school calendar changes (summer, Tết, exam periods).
