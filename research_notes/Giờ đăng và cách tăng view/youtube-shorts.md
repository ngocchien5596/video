# YouTube Shorts: how recommendations work and upload best practices (2025–2026)

Labels used: **[OFFICIAL]** = YouTube Help, YouTube Blog, YouTube staff (Creator Insider, Liaison, CEO). **[3P]** = third-party analytics firm, blog or news coverage. **[INFERENCE]** = my reasoning, not stated by a source.
Research date: 2026-11-10. Context: Vietnamese-language animated Shorts with AI-voiced puppet characters.

## 1. How the Shorts feed recommends videos, and how views are counted

### Takeaway
YouTube's official line is that Shorts and long-form use the same satisfaction-based approach: "the audience is the algorithm". A new Short goes to a small group of viewers likely to enjoy it. Reach grows or tapers off depending on how they respond, mainly whether they watch or swipe away, plus retention and engagement. YouTube does not publish signal weights. Public Shorts views have counted every play and replay since 31 Mar 2025, and every format uses the same rule since 24 Aug 2026. **Engaged views** are the old, stricter metric, and they are what drive YPP eligibility and earnings. Since Oct 2026, YouTube has said it will cut reach for unoriginal or re-uploaded Shorts.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] Rene Ritchie (YouTube Liaison, YouTube Blog, 2024): the Shorts and long-form algorithms are "at the core… the same" and focus on viewer satisfaction. Shorts get broader exploration because swiping away is cheap: "if the system tries something a little different, and you love it, great! If not, you swipe away, no worries!" — [YouTube Blog, Rene's Top Five, Apr 2024](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/renes-top-five-on-youtube-april-11-2024-edition/)
- [OFFICIAL] Todd Sherman (Shorts product lead), Creator Insider, Aug 2023: YouTube finds a set of people who may enjoy the video, and reach "grows or tapers off" based on their response. "The audience is the algorithm." Early views for new creators are "exploratory". The host (Ritchie) called early Shorts views "more like impressions than traditional views". — [Creator Insider video, t=388s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n3jsYK_-aRU&t=388s), summarized at [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- [OFFICIAL] Ritchie: "The algorithm follows the audience, so please the audience." Use the Audience tab to see which videos grow your audience, then make more like those. He has a Shorts Q&A series with Todd Beaupré (Senior Director, Growth & Discovery), Aug 2024. — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/08/26/rene-ritchie-shorts-creator-faqs/)
- [OFFICIAL] The "How many chose to view" metric (Viewed vs. Swiped away) is the percentage of feed presentations where viewers watched rather than swiped away. It replaces click-through rate for the Shorts feed. — definitions via [Bytecap synthesis of YouTube Help](https://www.bytecap.io/research/youtube-shorts-retention-benchmarks) and [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/youtube-algorithm/)
- [OFFICIAL] Satisfaction matters beyond raw watch time: YouTube aims for viewers to feel their time was well spent. — attributed to Ritchie in [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/blog/youtube-algorithm) (secondary paraphrase)
- [OFFICIAL] YouTube has not published Shorts signal weights. — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/). I found no official source that ranks likes, shares, subscribes, loops or surveys against each other.
- [OFFICIAL] **31 Mar 2025 view-count change** (announced 26 Mar 2025):
  - Shorts views now count every time a Short starts to play or replay, with no minimum watch time. Before this, a few seconds of watching were needed.
  - The old metric was renamed **"engaged views"**. It sits in YouTube Analytics under Advanced Mode and shows how many viewers chose to keep watching.
  - YPP eligibility and Shorts revenue share still use engaged views.
  - YouTube recommends using engaged views when comparing Shorts published before 31 Mar 2025 with later ones.
  - Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/26/youtube-is-changing-how-youtube-shorts-views-are-counted), [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/03/26/youtube-shorts-views-counting-stats/), [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-changes-how-shorts-views-are-counted-from-march-31/)
- [OFFICIAL] **24 Aug 2026 change**: "Beginning August 24, 2026, views are counted the moment a video starts to play across all formats" (Shorts, VOD, live). It "won't impact your YouTube Partner Program (YPP) earnings or eligibility". Earnings stay based on "engaged views" and "engaged watch hours", and eligibility on "qualified views". — [YouTube Help 2991785](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2991785?hl=en)
- [3P] Agentio estimated that public views overstate engaged views by about 1.67x, and by more on small channels. This is a single-firm estimate. — reported via [search summary of hellothematic / contentiq coverage](https://contentiq.media/learn/youtube-view-count-change). The exact announcement date is reported as 17 or 19 Aug 2026; sources conflict.
- [OFFICIAL] **1 Oct 2026 originality update** (Ritchie, Creator Insider, with a Help Community post titled "Prioritizing original content on Shorts"):
  - YouTube is updating recommendations to "further prioritize original" Shorts and reduce reach for channels "primarily aggregating or re-uploading other creators' videos without significant changes".
  - Adding value does **not** mean "just VO descriptions of what's happening on screen, minor technical edits, or template-based bulk changes".
  - Channels that shift toward original content get their reach re-evaluated.
  - YouTube gave no start date, no re-evaluation timeline and no dashboard indicator.
  - Sources: [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-original-shorts-reposted-clips-reach/591774/), [YouTube Help Community post](https://support.google.com/youtube/blog/470890423/prioritizing-original-content-on-shorts?hl=en), [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-updates-shorts-algorithm-to-put-more-focus-on-original-content/831976)
- [OFFICIAL] **15 Jul 2025 YPP policy change**: "repetitious content" was renamed "inauthentic content" to better catch mass-produced or repetitive videos, including template-based uploads with little variation, "regardless of whether AI… was used". Ritchie called it a "minor update" because such content was already ineligible for monetization. Using AI to improve content is still allowed. Realistic synthetic content must be disclosed. — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/07/03/youtube-crackdown-repetitive-non-original-content/amp/), [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/youtube-clarifies-july-15-policy-update-repetitious-content-rebranded-as-inauthentic-content/), [iPhoneInCanada](https://www.iphoneincanada.ca/2025/07/10/youtube-monetization-policies-combating-ai-content/)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] Because public views now count every play, the public view count and the basic "views" number say little about quality. The metrics to optimize are engaged views, the share of viewers who chose to view, and average percentage viewed.
- [INFERENCE] For AI-voiced puppet animation, the two policy risks are the Oct 2026 reach cut for unoriginal content and the "inauthentic content" YPP rule. Keep each episode clearly distinct: original scripts, varied scenes and storylines, recurring characters with real narrative. Avoid template-swapped near-duplicates such as the same scene with only the words changed. Disclosure of synthetic content is aimed at *realistic* depictions, so cartoon puppets likely don't need it, but check the current Help page.
- [INFERENCE] Loops count as extra public views. A seamless loop can also lift average percentage viewed above 100%. Treat this as a retention tactic, not an officially confirmed ranking boost.

### Gaps
- I found no official statement ranking likes, shares, subscribes, comments or satisfaction surveys for Shorts specifically, and no official confirmation that loops or rewatches are a ranking signal beyond retention.
- I could not find a primary Todd Beaupré statement specific to Shorts swipe rate.

## 2. Shorts length (up to 3 minutes)

### Takeaway
Since 15 Oct 2024, Shorts can run up to 3 minutes (square or vertical aspect ratio). YouTube's official position is that there is no ideal length: use the length the story needs. Benchmarks for retention by length come only from third parties, and YouTube publishes no thresholds.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] The 3-minute maximum has applied since 15 Oct 2024 for square or taller videos. Videos shot before then are not affected. — [Engadget](https://engadget.com/entertainment/youtube/youtube-shorts-can-now-run-up-to-three-minutes-160002081.html), [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-expands-3-minute-shorts-to-all-users/736967/)
- [OFFICIAL] Todd Sherman: there is no ideal length; use the length the story needs. — [Creator Insider t=183s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n3jsYK_-aRU&t=183s)
- [3P] Shorts between 1 and 3 minutes that contain Content ID-claimed copyrighted material may be blocked. Removing the claimed content makes them playable again, with no penalty. This is unconfirmed by an official source I could find. — [ZoneofGenius newsletter](https://zoneofgenius.beehiiv.com/p/youtube-shorts)
- [3P] YouTube publishes no universal "good retention" threshold. Compare each Short against your own Shorts in the same length band (15–30s, 30–60s, 60s+). — [Bytecap](https://www.bytecap.io/research/youtube-shorts-retention-benchmarks)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] Longer Shorts usually have a lower average percentage viewed, so a 2–3 minute Short needs a real narrative hook every few seconds. For puppet-story content, test 30–60s episodes against 90–180s ones and compare engaged views and chose-to-view within each length band.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable large-sample study on length versus reach in 2025–2026.

## 3. Title, description, hashtags, language, captions

### Takeaway
Hashtags (including #shorts) are officially **not required**. More than 60 hashtags causes YouTube to ignore all of them. Titles matter mainly for search, the subscriptions and channel surfaces, and as on-screen context. Keep 1–3 relevant hashtags and use clear keywords in the title in the audience's language.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] On hashtags, Sherman said: "Are they required? No, of course not." — [Creator Insider t=293s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n3jsYK_-aRU&t=293s)
- [OFFICIAL] "If a video or playlist has more than 60 hashtags, we'll ignore each hashtag on that content." — [YouTube Help 6390658](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en-GB)
- [3P] Only the first few hashtags (reportedly 3) display near the title. Blogs recommend 3–5 relevant hashtags. Some say hashtags in Shorts give "minimal algorithmic lift". — [Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/youtube-shorts-hashtags-virality), [Circleboom](https://circleboom.com/blog/how-many-hashtags-should-i-use-on-youtube-shorts/)
- [3P] Sources disagree on whether hashtags belong in the title or the description. — [vaizle](https://insights.vaizle.com/best-hashtags-for-youtube-shorts/), [Subscribr titles & thumbnails](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/youtube-shorts-titles-thumbnails-matter)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] For Vietnamese content:
  - Write titles in Vietnamese with natural search phrases, such as the character name plus the topic.
  - Set the video language and title/description language to Vietnamese so YouTube serves the Short to Vietnamese-speaking viewers.
  - Burn in Vietnamese on-screen captions, because many viewers watch muted and captions help retention.
  - Upload an SRT file too, for accessibility and auto-translation.
  - None of this is specifically confirmed for Shorts ranking.

### Gaps
- I found no official 2025–2026 statement on whether the title or description text affects how the Shorts feed (as opposed to search) distributes a video.
- I found no official guidance on language-setting effects or caption effects for Shorts.

## 4. Thumbnail / cover

### Takeaway
Until July 2026 you could only pick a frame from the video (on mobile, and later from three suggested frames on desktop). Since **24–25 Jul 2026**, YPP creators can upload **custom thumbnails** for Shorts, without A/B testing. YouTube's own position is that the thumbnail is mostly invisible in the feed. It matters on channel pages, search, Shorts shelves and the subscriptions feed.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] Neal Mohan: "Custom thumbnails for Shorts are rolling out to all @YouTube Partner Program creators." The rollout began around 24 Jul 2026. Desktop also offers three suggested frames, mobile lets you pick any frame, and thumbnails can be changed retroactively in Studio. — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/07/24/youtube-shorts-custom-thumbnails-neal-mohan-feature-update/), [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2026/7/youtube-introduces-custom-thumbnails-for-shorts-and-ask-studio-ai-thumbnail-generation/)
- [3P] The feature excludes A/B testing ("Test & Compare"). — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-ends-2-year-wait-for-shorts-thumbnails-but-blocks-a-b-testing/)
- [OFFICIAL] Sherman: "Most thumbnails are never seen because people are in the feed." — [Creator Insider t=260s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n3jsYK_-aRU&t=260s)
- [3P] Shorts thumbnails are vertical, not 16:9. — [Tubefilter search summary](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/07/24/youtube-shorts-custom-thumbnails-neal-mohan-feature-update/)
- [3P] I found no reliable data showing that custom Shorts thumbnails raise reach. — [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/social-media-marketing/youtube-shorts-custom-thumbnails-no-ab-testing/)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] The first frame is effectively the "thumbnail" in the feed. Open on an expressive character close-up with motion or text, not a title card or a fade-in. Still set a clean cover (a character face plus 2–4 words of Vietnamese text) for channel-page and search browsing.

### Gaps
- Platform details for custom uploads (desktop versus mobile) are inconsistent across reports.

## 5. Audio: trending sounds, original audio, music copyright, remix

### Takeaway
I found little official 2025–2026 material in this pass. The Oct 2026 originality update says remixes must add real value: original commentary, analysis or storytelling. Copyrighted music in Shorts longer than 1 minute may be blocked by Content ID (third-party claim).

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] On remixing another creator's clip, value must be added. Voice-over descriptions, minor edits or templated bulk changes do not count. To share another creator's video, YouTube suggests using the Posts tab. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-original-shorts-reposted-clips-reach/591774/)
- [3P] Shorts of 1–3 minutes containing claimed copyrighted content may be blocked. — [ZoneofGenius](https://zoneofgenius.beehiiv.com/p/youtube-shorts)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] For an original animated series, original voice and audio is low-risk. If you use music, choose YouTube's Shorts audio library or royalty-free tracks, especially for Shorts longer than 60s. Using a trending sound may help discovery through the sound's page, but I found no official confirmation that it boosts ranking.

### Gaps
- I found no official statement on whether trending or licensed sounds get preferential distribution.
- I did not verify the current licensing limits for Shorts audio library tracks on Shorts longer than 60s.

## 6. Early performance, testing, re-pushes, deleting and re-uploading

### Takeaway
Officially, YouTube tests each Short with a small, likely-interested audience and widens or narrows reach based on response. Posting time of day isn't optimized for (news excepted). Deleting and re-uploading risks being treated as spam. Officially, a weak start isn't fatal: "learn from it and… try again". Delayed or later surges are widely reported by creators, but I found no official confirmation of a "re-push" mechanism.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] Seed audience: reach grows or tapers off depending on response. — [Creator Insider t=388s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n3jsYK_-aRU&t=388s)
- [OFFICIAL] YouTube does not optimize for time of day. — [Creator Insider t=327s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n3jsYK_-aRU&t=327s)
- [OFFICIAL] On deleting and reposting: "There is a risk there that that gets seen as spam in our systems." — [Creator Insider t=443s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n3jsYK_-aRU&t=443s)
- [OFFICIAL] "If that didn't go how you wanted to go, learn from it and move forward and try again." — [Creator Insider t=458s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n3jsYK_-aRU&t=458s)
- [3P] Creator guides say a weak early "viewed" rate limits distribution. YouTube has not confirmed this as a mechanism. — [search summary of creator guides, e.g. Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/youtube-shorts-analytics-metrics-viral)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] The first hour matters only because that is when the seed audience responds. No official "golden hour" threshold exists. Don't delete underperformers. Re-cut them into a meaningfully new video instead, because a near-duplicate also risks the inauthentic and unoriginal content rules.

### Gaps
- I found no official data on Shorts resurfacing weeks later.

## 7. Comments, related video link, playlists, end screens, community posts

### Takeaway
The official linking tool is **"Related video"**: one clickable link under the channel handle in the Shorts player. It needs advanced feature access, and the target must be a public or unlisted video, Short or live that meets the Community Guidelines. Shorts have no end screens (this pass did not re-verify that). The Posts tab is the official recommended way to share other creators' videos.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] You can add a related video after upload, in Studio under Content. It shows as a clickable link below your channel handle. "Only available with advanced feature access." The target must be public or unlisted. — [YouTube Help 14075157](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157?hl=en)
- [3P] The publishing API reportedly can't set the related video, so it has to be added manually in Studio. Sources disagree on whether the target must be on the same channel. — [Opus help](https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/link-shorts-to-original-video), [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-related-links-connect-shorts-long-videos/)
- [OFFICIAL] Use the Posts tab to point to other creators' videos instead of re-uploading them. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-original-shorts-reposted-clips-reach/591774/)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] Link each episode to the previous or next episode, or to a playlist-style compilation, using Related video. Group episodes in a playlist or series. Pin a comment asking a story question to prompt replies. Replying to comments helps build community, but I found no source showing it improves Shorts ranking.

### Gaps
- I found no official statement on pinned comments, replies or playlists as Shorts ranking factors.
- I did not re-verify in this pass that Shorts lack end screens.

## 8. Upload frequency and consistency

### Takeaway
Officially, there is no posting-frequency threshold that unlocks reach. Posting several Shorts per day isn't penalized per se, but mass-produced, template-like output risks the July 2025 inauthentic-content rule (monetization) and the Oct 2026 originality rule (reach).

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] On daily posting thresholds, Sherman said: "There is nothing in our systems" that unlocks reach for hitting a target. — [Creator Insider t=345s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n3jsYK_-aRU&t=345s)
- [OFFICIAL] The inauthentic content policy covers mass-produced, repetitive, template-based uploads. — [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/youtube-clarifies-july-15-policy-update-repetitious-content-rebranded-as-inauthentic-content/)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] Pick a sustainable pace, such as 1 Short per day or a few per week, where each episode is distinct. Quality per Short beats volume, especially for an AI-produced series.

### Gaps
- I found no official or large third-party study showing a reach penalty for multiple Shorts per day.

## 9. Shorts analytics in YouTube Studio

### Takeaway
Watch these metrics:
- **Chose to view** (viewed vs. swiped away)
- **Engaged views**
- **Average view duration** and **average percentage viewed**
- The **retention graph**, especially the first 1–3 seconds and any drop points
- Subscribers gained per Short (Audience tab)

YouTube publishes **no official benchmarks**. Third-party figures vary widely, so benchmark against your own channel within the same length bands.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL / 3P synthesis] YouTube publishes no universal good-retention threshold. Compare against similar-length, similar-topic Shorts on your channel over a consistent time window. — [Bytecap (updated Jul 2026)](https://www.bytecap.io/research/youtube-shorts-retention-benchmarks)
- [3P] "Viewed vs swiped away" is under Analytics > Content > Shorts. Creator guides cite target viewed rates of 70–80% or more, but these figures are unofficial and inconsistent. — [creatoressentials glossary](https://www.creatoressentials.com/glossary/viewed-vs-swiped-away/), [ContentStudio](https://contentstudio.io/blog/youtube-shorts-analytics-guide), [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/youtube-shorts-analytics/)
- [OFFICIAL] Engaged views are under Advanced Mode. Use them to compare across the Mar 2025 and Aug 2026 counting changes. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/26/youtube-is-changing-how-youtube-shorts-views-are-counted), [YouTube Help 2991785](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2991785?hl=en)
- [OFFICIAL] Ritchie: use the Audience tab to find the videos that grow your audience. — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/08/26/rene-ritchie-shorts-creator-faqs/)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] A practical checklist per Short, at 48h and 7d:
  1. Chose to view versus your channel median.
  2. Average percentage viewed versus the median for its length band.
  3. Retention dip location: under 3s means a hook problem, mid-video means a pacing problem.
  4. Ratio of engaged views to views.
  5. Subscribers per 1,000 engaged views.

### Gaps
- I found no credible large-sample 2025–2026 benchmark from vidIQ, TubeBuddy, Buffer, Hootsuite or Socialinsider in this pass. The cited third-party numbers are blog-level estimates.
