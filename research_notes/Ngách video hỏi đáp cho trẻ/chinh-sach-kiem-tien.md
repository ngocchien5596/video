# Platform policies, legal rules and monetization for kids-oriented educational short videos (YouTube Shorts + TikTok), Vietnam-based creator

Research date: 2026-11-10. 17 tool calls in total. Primary Google Help pages were fetched directly. Most TikTok and Vietnamese-law material comes from secondary sources, and each item below says so.

## 1. YouTube "Made for Kids" (MFK): criteria, disabled features, effect on Shorts reach and revenue, quality principles

### Takeaway
MFK is a legal designation under COPPA that every creator must set, wherever they live. It turns off personalized ads, comments, notifications, the miniplayer, playlist saving and more. Ads become contextual only, which creator reports put at a much lower RPM. YouTube's "quality principles for kids and family content" decide whether MFK content gets recommended, gets into YouTube Kids and gets monetized, and channels focused on low-quality kids content can be suspended from YPP.

### Cited Findings
- **Criteria:** content is MFK if children are the primary audience, or if it is "mixed audience" content where children are one audience among others. YouTube weighs subject matter, intended audience (metadata included), child actors or models, appealing characters or toys, language, activities, songs or stories, other audience evidence, and whether the content is advertised to children. Family-friendly content, activities traditionally associated with children, and content kids might happen to see do NOT automatically qualify. In the US a "kid" is under 13; other countries may define it differently. The page shows no last-updated date (it says "©2026 Google") and cites the FTC's November 2019 blog post. — [YouTube Help: Determining if your content is made for kids](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9528076?hl=en)
- **Mislabeling:** "Improper settings may result in consequences on YouTube and legal consequences under COPPA and other laws." The creator is responsible for the designation, and YouTube's systems may not catch everything the FTC considers made for kids. — [YouTube Help 9528076](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9528076?hl=en)
- **Features restricted on MFK videos:** home-feed autoplay, cards and end screens, video watermarks, channel memberships, comments, donate button, live chat (and live chat donations), merch and ticketing, notification alerts, **personalized ads**, **miniplayer**, Super Chat/Super Stickers, **adding to playlists / Watch Later**. **Restricted on MFK channels:** channel memberships, notifications, posts, comments. Some ad categories can still serve. — [YouTube Help: Features restricted on made for kids content (9632097)](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9632097?hl=en)
- The page says nothing about Shorts specifically, including Shorts remix. — [YouTube Help 9632097](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9632097?hl=en)
- MFK videos "are more likely to be recommended alongside other kids' videos." — [YouTube Help 9528076](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9528076?hl=en)
- **Shorts Super Thanks** requires that the Short is not set to MFK. — reported in search results summarizing [YouTube Shorts monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en). I did not see this condition in my direct fetch of that page; it most likely comes from a separate Super Thanks help page.
- **Shorts revenue sharing:** the official Shorts monetization policies page does not mention MFK at all. Eligibility depends on accepting the Shorts Monetization Module, following the advertiser-friendly guidelines, and posting original content; payouts are based on "engaged views." Creators keep 45% of their allocated Creator Pool revenue. — [YouTube Help: Shorts monetization policies (12504220)](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en)
- **NEW (announced on that page):** "Starting February 1, 2027," a creator needs **≥10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days** to earn from the Creator Pool and YouTube Premium Shorts revenue each month. The 90-day window ends on the 15th of the preceding month. Also from that date, targeted Shorts ads aimed at 5 or fewer channels pay those channels directly at a 45% share. — [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en)
- **Non-original Shorts are ineligible:** unedited clips of others' movies or TV, reuploads, and "compilations with no original content added." — [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en)
- **RPM (anecdotal only):** creator blogs report MFK RPMs of about **$0.50–$2**, against **$3–$15** for adult-targeted content, because MFK ads are non-personalized and contextual. — [Subscribr: Made for Kids monetization rules](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/youtube-made-for-kids-monetization-rules); [vidIQ: Made for Kids YouTube, how to make money in 2026](https://www.vidiq.com/blog/post/make-money-kids-youtube-channel). These are not official figures.
- Third-party blogs disagree on how MFK Shorts are treated. One says MFK Shorts remain eligible for Shorts revenue sharing; another says they earn at the lower contextual rate and drop out of the personalized Shorts shelf. — [vidIQ](https://www.vidiq.com/blog/post/make-money-kids-youtube-channel); [Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/youtube-made-for-kids-monetization-rules)
- **Quality principles for kids and family content:**
  - **High quality:** looking after yourself and others; learning and inspiring curiosity (school subjects, fun facts, encouraging kids to ask questions); creativity, play and imagination; life skills and experiences (clear story arc and takeaway); showing kids the world.
  - **Low quality:** heavily promotional; encouraging negative behaviors; **deceptively educational** (titles or thumbnails promise learning but the video delivers incorrect or unhelpful information); **hard to follow** (jumbled storylines, unclear audio, no clear beginning, middle and end, "often from mass production or autogeneration"); sensational or misleading, including keyword stuffing; strange use of children's characters.
  - **Effects:** high-quality MFK content is raised in recommendations, and the principles guide inclusion in YouTube Kids and monetization. A strong focus on low-quality MFK content may mean YPP suspension, and individual videos may get limited or no ads. — [YouTube Help: Quality principles for kids and family content (10774223)](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10774223?hl=en)
- The YPP channel monetization policies currently enforce six low-quality principles, and YouTube may add more. — [YouTube Help: YPP channel monetization policies (1311392)](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)

### Inferences
- For a Q&A or educational kids channel, the "learning and inspiring curiosity" principle is the target. The two biggest risks are "deceptively educational" (factual errors) and "hard to follow / autogenerated." Fact-checking and a clear question → answer structure matter for monetization, not just for quality.
- MFK Shorts lose comments, notifications and playlist saving. Comments are an engagement signal for the Shorts feed, so reach outside the kids recommendation cluster will probably be weaker (inference; YouTube has not confirmed it).
- The February 2027 threshold of 10M Shorts views in 90 days will exclude most small channels from the Creator Pool. A new Vietnamese kids channel should not plan around Shorts ad revenue until it is far past that level. Long-form videos, sponsorships or other income will matter more.

### Gaps
- Whether Shorts remix (Green Screen, Cut, Collab) is disabled for MFK Shorts is not stated on the pages I fetched. My recollection is that it is disabled, but I could not verify that from a source.
- No official YouTube figure for MFK RPM or CPM in Vietnam. All RPM numbers are anecdotal and US-centric. Vietnamese-language viewers generally earn low RPMs (unverified).
- No current official page fetched on YouTube Kids app eligibility beyond "quality principles guide inclusion."

## 2. Mixed-audience / family content and the risks of mislabeling

### Takeaway
"Aimed at parents" is legitimate only if children really are not the primary or a meaningful audience. YouTube's own definition counts mixed-audience content (children as one of several audiences) as MFK. Recent FTC enforcement shows that mislabeling carries real penalties.

### Cited Findings
- Mixed-audience content, where children are one audience among others, is treated as MFK under YouTube's definition. — [YouTube Help 9528076](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9528076?hl=en)
- Family-friendly content is not automatically MFK, and general-audience content should be marked "not made for kids." — [YouTube Help 9528076](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9528076?hl=en)
- **FTC v. Disney (September 2025):** Disney agreed to pay **$10 million** because it did not label some YouTube videos MFK, which allowed data to be collected from viewers under 13 and used for targeted ads (COPPA Rule). It reportedly failed to re-label about 300 videos in 2020, after YouTube had warned about the designations. Disney must also run an MFK review program, which is waived if YouTube implements age assurance. — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/disney-pay-10-million-settle-ftc-complaint-collection-childrens-person-rcna228786); [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/disney-10-million-fine-ftc-complaint-made-for-kids-youtube/)
- Google/YouTube paid $170M in the 2019 FTC/NY AG settlement, which created the creator-designation system. — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/disney-pay-10-million-settle-ftc-complaint-collection-childrens-person-rcna228786)

### Inferences
- A Q&A channel answering "why is the sky blue?" questions with cartoon characters, simple language and child-appealing visuals for 4–10 year-olds is almost certainly MFK under YouTube's factors, even if the metadata says "for parents."
- A channel can be honestly "not made for kids" only if parents or teens are the real target: parenting tips, "how to explain X to your kid," adult narration speaking to the parent, adult-style fun facts.
- FTC jurisdiction over a Vietnam-based individual is practically limited. YouTube enforces the designation globally, though, and can override it or penalize the channel (the help page warns of "consequences on YouTube").

### Gaps
- I found no published FTC action against a small non-US creator. The enforcement risk for a Vietnamese individual is uncertain and is mainly platform-side.

## 3. YouTube inauthentic / mass-produced content policy (July 2025) and AI disclosure

### Takeaway
Since 15 July 2025, YPP's "repetitious content" policy has been called **"inauthentic content"** and explicitly covers mass-produced, templated and AI-template content. It applies channel-wide. AI disclosure labels became more prominent and partly automatic in May 2026.

### Cited Findings
- On **15 July 2025** YouTube renamed "repetitious content" to "inauthentic content," covering repetitive or mass-produced content. The reused content policy was not changed. — [YouTube Help 1311392](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
- **Not allowed:**
  - content that "looks like it's made with a template";
  - "Videos where characters are put in the same situation over and over again with the same outcome";
  - AI-generated content that uses generic templates and gives "the impression of mass production."
- **Allowed:** a similar pattern with "a distinct storyline, focus, or concept," such as the same intro and outro but different main content. — [YouTube Help 1311392](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
- Monetization "may be removed from your entire channel." — [YouTube Help 1311392](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
- YouTube's Creator Liaison Rene Ritchie called it a "minor update" to a long-standing rule. — [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/youtube-clarifies-monetisation-policy-targeting-inauthentic-content-9493155); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-clarifies-inauthentic-content-policy-changes/)
- **AI disclosure:** creators must disclose realistic altered or synthetic content, meaning content a viewer could mistake for a real person, place or event. — [YouTube Blog: Improving AI labels](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/improving-ai-labels-viewers-creators/); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/companies/youtube-mandates-labelling-of-synthetic-ai-generated-content)
- **May 2026 update:**
  - The label for photorealistic or meaningfully AI-altered content moved directly below the player.
  - Unrealistic or animated content keeps the label in the expanded description.
  - YouTube now auto-applies the label when it detects significant photorealistic AI use and the creator has not disclosed it.
  — [MobileSyrup, 27 May 2026](https://mobilesyrup.com/2026/05/27/youtube-ai-labelling-more-visible/); [Minimatters](https://minimatters.com/youtube-ai-content-labeling-update-in-may-2026/); [DesignRush](https://news.designrush.com/youtube-ai-labels-auto-detection)
- **AI and kids:** a child-advocacy coalition asked YouTube to ban AI-generated content on YouTube Kids. YouTube said it was developing labels for Kids, but the current labels apply only to main YouTube, not the YouTube Kids app. — secondary coverage in search results ([Minimatters](https://minimatters.com/youtube-ai-content-labeling-update-in-may-2026/), [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-shifts-generative-ai-labels-to-spots-viewers-will-actually-see/)). I did not verify this against a primary YouTube statement.
- The kids quality principles name "hard to follow … often from mass production or autogeneration" as low quality. — [YouTube Help 10774223](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10774223?hl=en)

### Inferences
- An AI-assisted Q&A kids channel built on one fixed template (same character, same voice, same format, only the question changes) sits close to the "inauthentic" line. Every video needs a distinct concept, real explanation value, human script or voice work, and varied visuals.
- Animated or cartoon AI visuals usually do not need the prominent photorealistic label, but disclosure in the description is still expected for meaningful AI use (inference from the May 2026 rules).

### Gaps
- No specific YouTube policy text dedicated to "AI kids content" was found beyond the quality principles and the general labeling rules.

## 4. TikTok: minimum age, kids content, Creator Rewards Program, AI labels, availability in Vietnam

### Takeaway
To earn from the Creator Rewards Program (CRP), creators must be 18 or older, have 10k followers and 100k views in 30 days, and post original videos **longer than 1 minute**. That rule rules out pure sub-60s Shorts-style clips. CRP availability in Vietnam is **unconfirmed or conflicting**. TikTok is a 13+ platform, so content aimed squarely at young children is a poor fit.

### Cited Findings
- CRP requirements in third-party guides: **18+** (ID-verified), **10,000 followers**, **100,000 views in the last 30 days**, original videos **longer than 1 minute**. Apply through TikTok Studio, where the option appears only when an account qualifies. Reported payouts are about **$0.40–$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views**, from creator reports; TikTok publishes no RPM. — [ttcalculator: CRP requirements 2026](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/creator-rewards-program/); [timetopost: CRP requirements 2026](https://timetopost.co/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-requirements-2026/); [Toptal](https://www.toptal.com/creator/post/how-to-join-the-tiktok-creator-rewards-program)
- **Vietnam availability, sources conflict:** some 2026 guides list Vietnam as eligible, while another says CRP is mostly in North America, the UK and Western Europe (one counts 8 countries). In 2023 TikTok's *Effect* Creator Rewards (for AR effect makers, a different program) expanded to Vietnam. — [multilogin](https://multilogin.com/blog/mobile/tiktok-creator-rewards-program/); [ttcalculator countries](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/creator-fund-countries/); [TechCrunch, Oct 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/10/11/tiktok-updates-effect-creator-rewards-lower-eligibility-requirements-new-payout-model-and-more)
- The official TikTok CRP support page (support.tiktok.com → tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7581821550694013452) renders client-side and could not be read. — [TikTok Support](https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7581821550694013452)
- TikTok in Vietnam: about 70 million users. Vietnamese creators are active, for example TikTok Awards Việt Nam on 18 October 2025, which added a "Storyteller of the Year" category. — [VietNamNet](https://vietnamnet.vn/en/tiktok-hits-70-million-users-in-vietnam-expands-regional-influence-2457692.html); [Advertising Vietnam](https://advertisingvietnam.com/article/tiktok-awards-viet-nam-2025-lan-dau-gioi-thieu-hang-muc-nguoi-ke-chuyen-cua-nam-p27233)

### Inferences
- If CRP is unavailable in Vietnam, TikTok income for a Vietnam-based creator would come from brand deals, TikTok Shop affiliate (kids books and educational toys; check that the Vietnamese program is open), LIVE gifts (18+), and pushing traffic to YouTube.
- Under-13 viewers cannot legally hold regular TikTok accounts, so a "kids Q&A" TikTok should be framed for parents or older kids and teens (13+). That also fits Vietnam's under-16 parental-account rule (see section 5).

### Gaps
- Not verified from TikTok primary sources:
  - the official CRP country list, and whether Vietnam is on it in late 2026;
  - TikTok's under-13 policy wording and the US-only "younger users" experience;
  - how TikTok's recommendation system treats content aimed at children;
  - TikTok's AI-generated content (AIGC) labeling rules and C2PA auto-labels.
- From memory and unverified: TikTok requires labeling realistic AIGC, auto-labels C2PA-tagged content, and removes unlabeled realistic AIGC. Confirm on the TikTok Community Guidelines "Edited Media and AI-Generated Content" section.

## 5. Vietnam law: Decree 147/2024, Children Law 2016, Decree 56/2017, educational content, creator tax

### Takeaway
Decree 147/2024/NĐ-CP (effective 25 December 2024) requires social-media accounts to be verified with a Vietnamese mobile number (or personal ID). Under-16s must use accounts registered by a parent or guardian. Children's privacy rules require parental consent, plus the child's own consent from age 7, before posting a child's private information or images. Platform income is taxable for Vietnamese residents, and the tax regime changed from 1 January 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **Decree 147/2024/NĐ-CP:** issued 9 November 2024, effective 25 December 2024. Cross-border and domestic social networks must verify user accounts with a Vietnamese mobile number. Some sources say a personal ID number is an alternative. Only verified accounts can post, comment or livestream. — [VOV](https://vov.vn/xa-hoi/tu-hom-nay-nguoi-dung-mxh-phai-xac-thuc-moi-duoc-dang-bai-binh-luan-post1144421.vov); [SGGP](https://www.sggp.org.vn/tu-hom-nay-25-12-xac-thuc-tai-khoan-mang-xa-hoi-bang-so-dien-thoai-di-dong-post774719.html); [LSVN](https://lsvn.vn/chinh-thuc-bat-buoc-xac-thuc-tai-khoan-mang-xa-hoi-bang-so-dien-thoai-a149711.html)
- **Under-16s:** they cannot register on their own. A parent or guardian registers with their own information and supervises what the child posts. — [LSVN](https://lsvn.vn/tu-15-12-2024-tre-duoi-16-tuoi-dang-ky-tai-khoan-mang-xa-hoi-bang-thong-tin-cua-cha-me-a149746.html); [Hệ thống pháp luật](https://hethongphapluat.com/phap-luat/chinh-sach-phap-luat/chinh-thuc-tre-em-choi-game-dung-mang-xa-hoi-phai-co-so-dien-thoai-cha-me-xac-thuc.html)
- Games: phone verification, and players under 18 are limited to 60 minutes per game per day. — same sources
- **Proposed 2026 amendment** to Decree 147, reported by a legal news site: under-16s would not be allowed to post, comment or share. This is only a draft and its status is unverified. — via search summary ([luatvietnam related news](https://luatvietnam.vn/bai-viet-lien-quan/147-2024-nd-cp-372523.html))
- **Children Law 2016 (Luật Trẻ em) and Decree 56/2017/NĐ-CP** (effective 1 July 2017):
  - Posting a child's private information online requires consent from a parent or caregiver; for children **aged 7 and over, the child's own consent is also required**.
  - Network service providers must provide warning tools and remove harmful content that infringes children's rights.
  - Hotline 111 handles reports.
  — [Hệ thống pháp luật (2017 article)](https://hethongphapluat.com/phap-luat/node/37868); Decree 56 articles 33–37 on children online, e.g. [Article 36](https://hethongphapluat.com/nghi-dinh-56-2017-nd-cp-huong-dan-luat-tre-em/dieu-36), [Article 37](https://hethongphapluat.com/nghi-dinh-56-2017-nd-cp-huong-dan-luat-tre-em/dieu-37)
- Article 54 of the Children Law: agencies and organizations are responsible for educating and protecting children online. — [Hải Phòng government training deck (PDF)](https://cdn.haiphong.gov.vn/gov-hpg/6811/tintuc/2025/8/5.-khung-chinh-sach-luat-phap-bvte-tren-moi-truong-mang.pptx638913623158690375.pdf)
- **Tax:**
  - Vietnamese residents must declare and pay tax on YouTube and TikTok income, including income already withheld abroad (foreign tax credit capped at the Vietnamese tax due).
  - Before 2026: revenue above 100 million VND/year was taxed at 5% VAT + 2% PIT on revenue (7% total).
  - From **1 January 2026**, under Decrees 68/2026 and 141/2026 (per the article), the flat 7% "will no longer apply across the board." The new method has three steps: check the revenue threshold, choose a calculation method, compute the tax.
  - Tax authorities cross-check bank data, including AdSense receipts.
  — [VnBusiness, 20 July 2026](https://vnbusiness.vn/quy-dinh-moi-tu-nam-2026-va-loi-canh-bao-cho-cac-youtuber.html); [Báo Lào Cai](https://baolaocai.vn/kiem-tien-tu-youtube-dong-thue-the-nao-tai-viet-nam-post897168.html); [MOF Q&A portal](https://portal.mof.gov.vn/hoidapcstc/home/cthoidap/163117)
- Google withholds US tax (up to 30% if tax info is not submitted) on US-view earnings for non-US creators. This is a Google rule from 2021, not Vietnamese law. — [doanhnghiephoinhap](https://doanhnghiephoinhap.vn/youtuber-viet-nam-se-phai-chiu-thue-30-cho-luot-xem-o-my.html)

### Inferences
- If the creator's own children (or any child) appear on camera, written parental consent is required, plus the child's consent if aged 7 or over. Animated or voice-only formats avoid most of that exposure.
- The creator's channel accounts must be verified with a Vietnamese phone number under Decree 147. That is routine, but it ties the account to the real identity used for tax.
- Submitting a W-8BEN in AdSense lowers US withholding under the treaty rate, if a Vietnam–US tax treaty is in force (I am not certain it is; see gaps).

### Gaps
- I did not read the full Decree 147 text: Article 23 versus Article 27 numbering conflicts between sources, and its content rules (the prohibited content list in Article 8 of the Law on Telecommunications/Internet) were not reviewed.
- Exact 2026 tax thresholds and rates under the new regime: the article does not give the new threshold. I recall that the revised Personal Income Tax Law (2025) raised the household-business threshold to 500 million VND/year, but I could not verify it here.
- Whether a Vietnam–US double-tax agreement is in force: the 2015 treaty was signed but, as I recall, not ratified. Unverified.
- I found no specific Vietnamese regulation on "educational content for children" on social media beyond the general children-protection and content rules.

## 6. 2025–2026 changes (age estimation, teen protections, other)

### Takeaway
YouTube began AI age estimation in the US in August 2025 and planned a wider rollout. The biggest upcoming monetization change for Shorts is the 10M-views/90-days Creator Pool threshold from 1 February 2027.

### Cited Findings
- YouTube announced US age-estimation rollout on 29 July 2025, with testing from 13 August 2025.
  - It uses signals such as activity and account age.
  - Users inferred to be teens get personalized ads disabled and wellbeing tools on.
  - Users can appeal with government ID, a selfie or a credit card.
  — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/29/youtube-rolls-out-age-estimatation-tech-to-identify-u-s-teens-and-apply-additional-protections); [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/news/youtube-ai-powered-technology-teen-users)
- February 2025 plans mentioned a global rollout later. — [Stuff.co.za](https://stuff.co.za/2025/02/14/youtube-plans-to-let-ai-independently-detect-whether-a-user-is-an-adult/)
- AI-label prominence and auto-labeling (May 2026). — see section 3
- Shorts Creator Pool threshold of ≥10M qualified views in 90 days from 1 February 2027. — [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en)
- Disney $10M COPPA settlement (September 2025), showing active FTC enforcement of MFK labeling. — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/disney-pay-10-million-settle-ftc-complaint-collection-childrens-person-rcna228786)
- Inauthentic content policy (15 July 2025). — see section 3

### Inferences
- If more viewers are detected as teens, fewer impressions carry personalized ads even on not-MFK family content watched by teens. This lowers RPM somewhat for teen-heavy audiences.

### Gaps
- No confirmed status of YouTube age estimation outside the US, including Vietnam, as of November 2026.
- I did not check for TikTok 2025–2026 teen-safety changes, such as under-16 default settings and screen-time limits.
